# Catan image assets

## Terrain and parchment

The six terrain images and the parchment texture come from:

- Repository: https://github.com/baige007/Catan-web-game-
- Paths: `public/assets/tiles/*.jpg` and `public/assets/backgrounds/background.jpg`
- Commit: `e64e296f1a8fc2ec92f7d84d16bf07ccf48be133` (2026-10-14)
- License: MIT, Copyright (c) 2025 Owen Sterling. The full licence is in `LICENSE`.

Each terrain image was centre-cropped to 1152×1152, resized to 512×512 and
encoded as WebP at quality 82. `parchment.webp` is a 900×1200 crop of the
background, resized to 512×512 and encoded as WebP at quality 80. The upstream
repository credits https://github.com/Erodenn/Simple-Catan as its starting
point.
