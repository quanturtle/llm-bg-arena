# Puerto Rico image assets

These PNGs are copied unchanged from:

- Repository: https://github.com/Middlederg/puerto-rico
- Path: `PuertoRico.Media/Resources/`
- Commit: `62234e4a44c76b04b8992d2cc7c5cded05b62135` (2023-07-17, "Initial")

The original Spanish filenames have been mapped to the game's English names and
grouped by kind:

- `buildings/` — building cards; `_1`, `_2`, `_3` suffixes are colonist variants
- `roles/` — role cards
- `goods/` — goods barrels
- `plantations/` — plantation and quarry tiles, plus their backs
- `tokens/` — doubloon coins
- `boards/` — island, city and trading-house reference boards

The upstream repository does not declare a license; confirm permission before
redistributing these images.
