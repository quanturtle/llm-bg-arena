# Tic-tac-toe

## Goal
Be the first to line up three of your marks in a straight line: across, up and down, or diagonal.

## Components
A 3x3 board of empty cells. P1 plays X, P2 plays O.

## Turn
P1 moves first, then players alternate. On your turn, put one mark on an empty cell.

## End and scoring
- Three of your marks in a line win at once: 1 point to the winner, 0 to the loser.
- If the board fills with no line, the game is a draw: 0.5 points each.

## Notation
A cell is its column letter followed by its row number. Columns run a, b, c from the left; rows run 1, 2, 3 from the bottom. `a1` is the bottom-left cell and `c3` the top-right.

An action is the cell you mark: `b2`.
